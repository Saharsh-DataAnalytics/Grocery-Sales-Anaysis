# Grocery-Sales-Anaysis
Power BI dashboard analyzing Grocery sales trends and business insight

## About This Project :
I worked on this project to understand how a grocery store is performing in terms of sales, profit, and customer orders. The goal was to explore the data and find meaningful insights that can help in making better business decisions.

## Key Highlights
- Total Orders: 9,994  
- Total Profit: 3.75M  
- Total Sales: 15M  


 ## What I Found
- The category **Eggs, Meat & Fish** brings in the highest sales, which means it’s a strong revenue driver.
- On the other hand, **Snacks** has the highest number of orders, showing it’s frequently purchased by customers.
- Profit has steadily increased from 2015 to 2018, which indicates overall business growth.
- Cities like **Kanyakumari** and **Tirunelveli** contribute the most in terms of total orders.
- Sub-categories such as **Health Drinks** and **Soft Drinks** are among the top contributors in order volume.

## My Understanding
One interesting thing I noticed is that high sales and high orders don’t always come from the same category. This means the business should not only focus on selling more items but also on selling the right items that generate better profit.


## What This Dashboard Shows
- Overall performance using key metrics like sales, profit, and orders  
- City-wise contribution to total orders  
- Category and sub-category performance  
- Year-wise profit trends  
- Interactive filters to explore data by year and category  

## Final Thought
From this analysis, I feel that the business can improve profitability by focusing more on high-value categories while maintaining consistent sales in high-order categories like snacks.
